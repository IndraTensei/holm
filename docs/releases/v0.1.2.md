Holm now makes way over your browser instead of sitting on its tabs.

## Download

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `Holm_0.1.2_x64-setup.exe` | Installer, about 2 MB, no administrator rights needed. Installing over an earlier version keeps your settings. |
| `Holm-0.1.2-portable-x64.exe` | The app on its own. Put it anywhere and run it. |

Holm runs on **Windows 10 (1809 or later) and Windows 11, 64-bit**. macOS and Linux are planned, not available
yet. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC". Choose **More info → Run
anyway**.

## What's changed

- **Make way** is the new default for Settings → General → Over a maximized window. Over a maximized app (a
  browser, say), you get the full island, until your pointer comes near it, on its way to a tab or the address
  bar. Then it drops to a thin line so whatever is under it gets the click, and it comes back when the pointer
  moves off. To open it from the line, push the pointer to the very top edge of the screen. Learn, Thin line
  and Full are still there.
- **Settings → About** has a link to [holm.indratensei.me](https://holm.indratensei.me).
- **README**: which systems Holm runs on, and how the pointer is read: a cheap position check 25 times a
  second (60 near the island), no mouse hook, no memory.
