The first release of Holm: a Dynamic Island for Windows 10 and 11 that stays small.

![Holm](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/IndraTensei/holm/main/docs/media/demo.gif)

## Download

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `Holm_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe` | Installer, about 2 MB, no administrator rights needed. You choose the folder, whether Holm starts when you sign in to Windows, and whether to add Start menu and desktop shortcuts. |
| `Holm-0.1.0-portable-x64.exe` | The app on its own. Put it anywhere and run it. |

Holm isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC". Choose **More info → Run
anyway**. Once it's running, Holm sits in the system tray. **Ctrl+Alt+Space** opens the island, and right-clicking
the tray icon opens Settings. You can turn **Start with Windows** on or off later in Settings → General.

If you used the earlier test builds called Tensei Island, quit them first. Holm brings over their settings and
start-with-Windows choice the first time it runs.

## What's in it

- **Music** from Spotify, browsers and any player in the Windows media card: cover, synced lyrics, seek, shuffle
  and repeat, the playing app's own volume, and an accent colour taken from the album art. With Pear Desktop
  you also get likes, dislikes and exact progress.
- **Claude Code**: live activity for each session, Allow or Deny permission requests from the island, a chime
  when a task finishes, and your 5-hour and weekly limits shown as a ring.
- **Harnesses**: shows the AI coding agents installed on your PC, which of them are running, and what each used
  today.
- **Games**: plays an intro when a game starts, stays out of the way while you play, and shows the agent news
  you missed once you're done.
- **Shelf**, clipboard history, a quick note, a focus timer, weather, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth toggles, and system
  readings.
- **Looks**: Notch, Pill or Line shapes, 15 themes plus your own, 13 typefaces, and three motion styles.
- **Light on your PC**: about 0.3% of one core and 116 MB at rest, with nothing moving while the island is
  hidden.

Requires Windows 10 1809 or later, or Windows 11, 64-bit. It also needs the WebView2 runtime, which Windows 11
already includes and the installer fetches if it's missing.
