Make way now waits until your pointer is right at the island.

## Download

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `Holm_0.1.4_x64-setup.exe` | Installer, about 2 MB, no administrator rights needed. Installing over an earlier version keeps your settings. |
| `Holm-0.1.4-portable-x64.exe` | The app on its own. Put it anywhere and run it. |

Holm runs on **Windows 10 (1809 or later) and Windows 11, 64-bit**. macOS and Linux versions are in
development ([#10](https://github.com/IndraTensei/holm/issues/10), [#11](https://github.com/IndraTensei/holm/issues/11)).
It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC". Choose **More info → Run
anyway**.

## Changed

- **Make way reacts only up close.** Over a maximized app, the island used to drop to a thin line as soon as
  the pointer came anywhere within 160 px either side or 120 px below it, so it stepped aside while you were
  working elsewhere near the top of the screen. Now it makes way only when the pointer is within 40 px either
  side or 48 px below it: the tabs and address bar right under it, not the whole area around it.
